Our Story

A clinic built on a quarter-century of care

White Pearl Clinics is founded by Prof. Wadih Sassine Nassif. After graduating from Lyon, France in Prosthodontics and Endodontics, Wadih returned to Lebanon and founded two dental clinics — in Akkar, North Lebanon, and in Jounieh, Mount Lebanon.

Over more than 25 years he has cared for over 5,000 patients and treated more than 100 multi-generation families, building a practice rooted in trust, precision, and the lasting confidence of a healthy smile.
